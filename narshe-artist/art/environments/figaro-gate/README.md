# Figaro Gate authored material commission

Generated delivery set for the Goldcrest / Figaro Gate battle skin. Opaque materials are delivered as lossless PNGs for predictable review; transparent plates use RGBA PNG.

## Assets

- sandstone-ashlar-v1.png — large warm amber ashlar courses.
- sandstone-brick-v1.png — finer amber brick courses for towers and gatehouse.
- courtyard-cobble-v1.png — worn ochre rounded courtyard setts.
- desert-sand-v1.png — pale tan low-frequency dune ripples.
- carpet-crimson-v1.png — crimson runner with gold side borders.
- roof-crimson-v1.png — crimson ribbed roof courses with gold accents.
- iron-dark-v1.png — blued near-black wrought iron.
- figaro-crest-v1.png — centered RGBA royal crest plate.
- banner-house-v1.png — 1024x2048 RGBA blue-and-crimson hanging banner plate.
- sky-goldenhour-v1.png — 2048x1024 golden-hour sky panorama.

## Prompt record

The seven material sheets were authored to the commission cores: flat orthographic painterly JRPG materials, broad readable shapes, warm amber / crimson / blue palette, no props or text, and no baked directional lighting. The crest and banner were authored as centered transparent plates, with the banner crest panel intentionally left plain for engine compositing. The sky is a flat painted blue-to-amber-to-cream gradient with a soft left-of-center sun glow.

The delivered sheets are normalized to the requested delivery sizes. The tileable sheets use periodic coordinate patterns so opposite edges are matched by construction.
