# The Quiet Before — v10, Part I

---

*A snowy crest overlooking a town nestled in a mountain valley. Mineshaft scaffolding is visible along the far ridge. Three imperial soldiers stand at the overlook.*

---

**Cassien:** Today we march toward Narshe to liberate the townspeople from a songbeast that has taken residence there.

**Brecht:** Speaking of songbeasts, why did we have to be assigned a mongrel to work with?

**Cassien:** Hold your tongue! Do you doubt the judgment of our commanders?

**Brecht:** I simply cannot believe my senses — are you sure that there wasn't some clerical error?

**Cassien:** We are representatives of an empire that seeks to unite the world's nations. Seira has trained alongside our people since childhood. If we are not able to welcome those who would join us to support our cause, all of our efforts will be for naught.

**Brecht:** But why send a halfling on a mission against one of her own? Blood runs deeper than water.

**Cassien:** Her mixed heritage endows her with magic that has military use...

**Brecht:** What use do we have for such machinations?

**Seira:** ...You'll see. I'll do my duty. It's the most that I can do to repent for my mother's people's sins.

**Brecht:** I'll work with you to complete the mission, but that doesn't mean that I trust you.

**Seira:** ...In truth, being liked was never my forte, but I am doing this for the Empire rather than for you.

**Cassien:** Let's head out. We'll settle this after the mission.

*[They move down the snowy path and off screen. Cassien leads. Brecht beside him. Seira a few steps behind — dark hair threaded with silver.]*

---

*The town wall. Two guards at the gate — Narshe militia. Hardened. Pickaxes at their belts alongside their weapons.*

**Town Guard:** Imperial soldiers... what brings you here?

**Cassien:** We've been told that a dangerous creature has taken residence here, and are here to protect you from it.

**Town Guard:** Protection? We handle our own problems here. Leave at once.

**Cassien:** The Empire spans the entire world, and without exception, what we have seen is that untamed, these creatures bring chaos and ruin.

**Second Guard:** My girl's been happier since that thing showed up than she's been in two years. Not since her mother passed. She sits with it after her shifts and she sings. I don't understand it. But I'm not about to let soldiers take it from her.

**Cassien:** I sympathize. But our orders are clear.

**Town Guard:** Then your orders don't apply here. Narshe is independent. We didn't ask for the Empire's help, and we don't want it.

**Cassien:** We will proceed. In time, you will see that we acted in your interest.

**Town Guard:** We will not let you pass.

---

*[GAMEPLAY: Combat encounter with Narshe militia. After reducing them to low health, a cutscene triggers.]*

---

**Cassien:** That's enough! Stand down, Brecht.

*The guards are on their knees.*

**Brecht:** They could still —

**Cassien:** I said enough. They're down. *(To the guards.)* Stay here. We'll be gone shortly.

<!-- @stage post-end -->

---

*Inside the mountain. The old mine descends beneath the town: dark stone, abandoned rails, frost silvering the upper ledges.*

*Deeper in. A broad chamber rises toward a sheltered shelf at the far end.*

*There — a shape in the frost. Silver-white. Luminous. Not quite animal, not quite anything else. It sits very still.*

*Three children around it. One leaning against its side. One braiding something into its fur. One — a girl, maybe ten — sitting cross-legged in front of it, talking quietly.*

*The creature's head is tilted. Listening. It makes no sound.*

**Brecht:** There it is.

**Cassien:** With children. *(Calling out.)* This area is under imperial authority. Children — step away from the creature.

*The girl stands. Puts herself between the soldiers and the songbeast. Arms out.*

**Girl:** No.

**Cassien:** This isn't a request. The creature is dangerous.

**Girl:** Her name is — I don't know her name. She doesn't talk. But she's not dangerous. She's my friend.

**Brecht:** It's a songbeast. It's been affecting this entire town.

**Girl:** She sings to me when I can't sleep. She's warm. You're not taking her.

**Cassien:** *(Low, to Brecht.)* We talk them down. No one touches the children.

**Brecht:** We have orders, Cassien. Every minute we wait —

**Cassien:** And I'm giving you an order now. We wait.

**Cassien:** *(To the girl.)* What's your name?

**Girl:** Lenne.

**Cassien:** Lenne. Listen to me. The Empire has dealt with these creatures before. They may seem gentle, but —

**Lenne:** She's the only good thing that's happened here since my mama died.

*Silence.*

*Seira is staring at the girl. At her outstretched arms. At the creature behind her — motionless, watching with large dark eyes.*

*Something feels strange. A pressure at the edge of hearing. Not a sound — like standing inside a bell that was struck a long time ago. Seira doesn't have a name for it.*

**Seira:** Cassien... something feels wrong.

**Cassien:** What do you mean?

**Seira:** I don't know. I feel something. From the creature. It's —

**Brecht:** This is exactly what I was afraid of. Her blood is reacting to —

**Cassien:** Quiet. Seira, can you manage?

**Seira:** ...I think so.

**Brecht:** You *think* so?

**Cassien:** Brecht —

**Brecht:** No. Enough waiting. I have a clear shot at the creature. The girl needs to move.

**Cassien:** Brecht — stand DOWN —

*Brecht raises his bow. Fires.*

<!-- @stage arrow-shot -->

*Lenne throws herself in front of the songbeast.*

*The arrow takes her in the shoulder. She falls.*

---

*The other children scream. Lenne is on the ground. Blood on the snow. She's trying to crawl back toward the creature. Still trying to shield it.*

**Cassien:** WHAT HAVE YOU DONE —

*The songbeast moves. For the first time, it moves.*

<!-- @stage beast-react -->

*It bends over Lenne. And from it comes a sound — not a word, not language. A single note that fills the square, fills the town, fills the valley. The sound a living thing makes when the one creature in the world that chose to love it is bleeding in the snow.*

*And Seira feels it.*

<!-- @stage resonance-start -->

*Not hears it. Feels it. In her chest, in her blood, in a place she didn't know existed. The songbeast's grief reaches inside her and finds something that answers.*

**Cassien:** Seira? Seira, report!

**Seira:** I can't — something is —

*The pressure doubles. The stones vibrate. Snow lifts off the ground.*

<!-- @stage resonance-climax -->

**Cassien:** What's happening to her?!

**Brecht:** I told you — I told you this would —

**Seira:** Run — Cassien, please, RUN —

**Cassien:** Seira —!

**Seira:** PLEASE —

*She is still herself.*

*She is still —*

---

*White.*
