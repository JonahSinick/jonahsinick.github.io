Battle music candidates — placeholder tracks
==============================================
Downloaded from OpenGameArt.org for evaluation as placeholder battle music
for a snowy-night tactics-RPG assault sequence. All three source files were
CC0 (public domain) at time of download, so no attribution is legally
required, but composer credit is included below as good practice.

Files were transcoded from the original WAV/FLAC to Ogg Vorbis (~192kbps)
with ffmpeg to reduce size; no other audio editing was done. candidateC's
source FLAC had an embedded cover-art image that ffmpeg would otherwise
have carried over as a spurious Theora "video" stream — it was stripped
during transcoding so the .ogg is audio-only.


battle1_candidateA.ogg
----------------------
Title:      "Determined Pursuit (epic orchestra loop)"
Composer:   Emma_MA
Source:     https://opengameart.org/content/determined-pursuit-epic-orchestra-loop
License:    CC0 (Public Domain) — no attribution required
Suggested credit line (optional): "Determined Pursuit" by Emma_MA (OpenGameArt.org), CC0
Length:     1:48 (108s)
Notes:      Frantic strings, determined horns, busy drums. Composer-tagged as
            explicitly loopable and intended for battle themes/boss fights.
            Has real forward momentum but leans more "energetic/urgent" than
            "cold and sparse" — closest match to the requested tempo range
            and the only one of the three confirmed loopable by the composer.

            would need trimming or a loop segment carved out of the middle.


RECOMMENDATION
==============
Recommend battle1_candidateA.ogg ("Determined Pursuit") as the primary pick:
it's the only one of the three with confirmed intentional looping, sits at
a length matching the brief (1:48, within the 1.5-3 min window), and has
real forward-driving orchestral momentum (strings/horns/percussion) without
being played for straightforward triumph. It is not a perfect match for the
"cold and sparse" description — it's busier/warmer than Narshe or Triangle
Strategy reference tracks — but of the three candidates it's the safest
functional placeholder for an actual battle-loop slot.

If mood match matters more than looping/functionality, battle1_candidateB
("Evil Approach") is the better fit for the dread/wrongness undertone, and
battle1_candidateC ("Frost in the Northern Winter") is the best fit for the
"cold, sparse, melancholy" descriptors — but neither was composer-verified
as loop-ready, and C is a genre mismatch (ambient vs. battle) that should be
confirmed by ear before use.


TRACKS CONSIDERED AND REJECTED
===============================
- "Battle in the Winter" by Johan Brodd (CC-BY 3.0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/battle-in-the-winter
  Title is a direct thematic match ("winter" + "battle"), but the composer's
  own description and site tags call it a "high paced classic piece,"
  "symphonic," "upbeat" — explicitly triumphant/majestic rather than cold or
  melancholy. Rejected per the brief's "NOT triumphant or bombastic" rule.

- "Laments of the War" by Cethiel (CC0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/laments-of-the-war
  Orchestral with choir and drums; "laments" suggested a melancholy fit, but
  the choir-forward arrangement pulls it further from the sparse
  string-ostinato/celesta texture described in the brief than the other CC0
  candidates. Downloaded and previewed but dropped in favor of the three
  files above.

- "Tactical Pursuit" and other Matthew Pablo battle tracks (CC-BY 3.0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/tactical-pursuit
  Described by the composer as aggressive/intense with rock/electronic
  elements (strings, choir, drumset) — tonally more action-movie than the
  cold minor-key orchestral brief. Not downloaded.

- "Siberian Intro" by Vehicle (CC0)
  https://opengameart.org/content/siberian-intro
  Short dramatic orchestral cue, likely intended as a stinger/intro rather
  than a loopable battle track. Not downloaded.
